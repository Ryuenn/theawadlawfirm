STEP-BY-STEP

The Process

01

Treatment

+

At the Awad Law Firm, we prioritize your health and well-being. The first step in our process is ensuring you receive the necessary medical treatment. We guide you in finding the right healthcare providers to address your injuries and ensure proper documentation of your treatment. Your recovery is our top priority.

02

Collecting Medical Records

+

Once your treatment is underway, we begin the critical process of collecting all medical records related to your injuries. Our team works directly with healthcare providers to gather comprehensive documentation, including medical bills, diagnostic reports, treatment plans, and physician notes to build the strongest possible case on your behalf.

03

Offer of Compromise

+

With all evidence and medical documentation in hand, we prepare a detailed demand package — known as an Offer of Compromise — to present to the insurance company. This comprehensive document outlines the full extent of your injuries, damages, and losses, establishing the value of your claim backed by irrefutable evidence.

04

Negotiation with Insurance Company

+

This is where our expertise truly shines. Our attorneys engage in strategic negotiations with the insurance company to secure the maximum compensation you deserve. We handle all communication, counter lowball offers, and leverage our thorough preparation to fight for a fair settlement that covers your medical expenses, lost wages, and pain and suffering.

05

Settlement & Disbursement

+

Once a fair settlement has been reached, we handle the final steps of disbursing your funds. We ensure all medical liens and outstanding bills are resolved, and the remaining balance is delivered to you promptly. Our team walks you through every detail of the settlement so you have complete transparency throughout the process.
