Karate Attorney Visits RCM to Speak on Bullying and Confidence

Karate Attorney recently visited RCM — Roswell Community Masjid — to speak with students about bullying and how to respond to it in a confident and respectful way.

Practical Strategies for Real Situations

During the presentation, he shared practical strategies on handling difficult situations, standing up for oneself, and supporting classmates who may be facing similar challenges. The session drew on real-world examples that students could immediately relate to and apply in their everyday school lives.

Open Conversation With Students

The session encouraged open conversation, allowing students to ask questions and share their thoughts about real situations they may encounter in school. Through discussion and examples, students learned how small actions — speaking up, showing empathy, and seeking help from trusted adults — can make a meaningful difference for themselves and for others around them.

Why Anti-Bullying Education Matters

Bullying affects many young people, and understanding how to respond to it is an important life skill. By learning how to handle these situations with confidence and respect, students gain tools that help them build stronger relationships and a healthier school environment. Empowering young people with these skills at an early age creates lasting change that extends well beyond the classroom.

A Positive and Lasting Impact

The visit was a positive and engaging experience for everyone involved, leaving students with valuable lessons about respect, courage, and the importance of looking out for one another. Karate Attorney looks forward to continuing these kinds of conversations with youth communities across Georgia.
