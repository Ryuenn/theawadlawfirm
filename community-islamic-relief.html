Islamic Relief Grand Iftar 2026: Belal Muhammad Autographed Gloves Spark Bidding War

Awad Law Firm was honored to attend Grand Iftar 2026 hosted by Islamic Relief USA, an organization known for its humanitarian work supporting communities in need around the world.

An Evening of Generosity and Reflection

The evening brought members of the community together for a meaningful gathering centered on generosity, reflection, and the shared spirit of giving during the holy month. Guests came together to support the important charitable initiatives led by Islamic Relief USA and to celebrate the values of compassion and service that define the organization's mission.

Belal Muhammad Joins the Celebration

A special highlight of the evening was the appearance of UFC fighter Belal Muhammad, who joined the event and shared in the celebration with attendees. His autographed gloves sparked an enthusiastic bidding war on the spot, driving meaningful support for Islamic Relief USA's charitable work and turning a memorable moment into a powerful act of giving.

The Karate Attorney Takes the Mic

The night also featured an interview conducted by Karate Attorney, adding a personal and engaging dimension to the evening that made the experience even more memorable for everyone involved.

The Power of Coming Together

Events like these remind us of the power of community, generosity, and coming together to support those in need. Awad Law Firm is grateful to have been part of such a meaningful evening and looks forward to continuing to show up wherever community and charitable work intersect.
